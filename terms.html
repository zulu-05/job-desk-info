Job Desk is a personal productivity application maintained for its owner’s use. These information pages do not offer a public hosted service, subscriptions or account registration.

Review and responsibility

The application helps organise information; it does not guarantee a vacancy’s availability, eligibility, a successful application, an apprenticeship placement or employment. Listings, travel information and automated suggestions can be incomplete or change. The user should check material details with the employer or relevant official service.

Application submissions, correspondence, offer decisions and factual claims in documents remain the user’s responsibility. Opening an application website is not confirmation that an application has been submitted.

Connected services

Optional services require the account holder’s permission and remain subject to their own terms, access limits and availability. Gmail authorisation can be withdrawn at any time. Any optional provider charges depend on the provider and the user’s chosen configuration; these information pages do not collect payments.

Data and contact

The privacy policy explains Google data access, local storage and disconnection. The owner should protect their computer and backups. Questions about Google authorisation can be directed to the support email shown on that authorisation screen.

Job Desk is not an employer, recruitment agency, apprenticeship provider or Google product.
